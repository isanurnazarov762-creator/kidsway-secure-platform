What a Guardian does

Picks up

Meets the child at home or school at the booked time, with the trip in the KidsWay app.

Travels with them

On foot, by public transport, taxi or car. The parent sees the trip live on the map.

Hands over safely

Only to the trusted person, who must give the one-time handover code.

Sends a photo report

One photo at the handover; the parent confirms and the trip is done.

Who can apply

  • Women aged 20 to 55. Guardians travel alone with young children, so this role is open to women only; age and gender are read from your MyKad number.
  • A Malaysian MyKad and a smartphone with mobile data.
  • Experience with children (family, school, kindergarten, tuition or volunteering).
  • Bahasa Malaysia or English; Chinese or Tamil is a plus.
  • A first aid certificate helps. If you do not have one, we arrange the course before your first trip.

How we check every Guardian

Parents trust us with their children, so every step below must be passed before a Guardian gets her first trip. Two different officers must approve.

  1. ApplicationPhone number confirmed by SMS, MyKad, two references.
  2. IdentityMyKad front and back, and a selfie, checked by an officer.
  3. e-DKK checkKidsWay asks JKM to check the register of sexual offences against children.
  4. Police recordCriminal record / good conduct check.
  5. ReferencesWe call both people you name.
  6. InterviewIn person, about you and how you work with children.
  7. First aidA valid first aid and CPR certificate.
  8. TrainingKidsWay training and a shadow trip with a senior Guardian.

The e-DKK and police checks are repeated every year.

How we protect your data

Encrypted

Your MyKad number and every document are encrypted on our server (AES-256-GCM).

Need to know

Only vetting officers can open your file. Each time they do, it is recorded.

Your choice

Withdraw at any time and everything you sent is erased at once.

Not kept forever

If your application is not successful, it is erased after 180 days.

Questions

How long does the process take?

Usually two to four weeks. The e-DKK and police checks take the longest. You can follow every step on this page.

Do I pay for the checks or the training?

No. KidsWay requests the checks and runs the training.

Why do you need my MyKad?

To confirm who you are and to request the e-DKK and police checks. It is never shown to parents.

How am I paid?

Per trip. The rates are explained at the interview.
